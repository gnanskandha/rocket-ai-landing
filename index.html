🚀 Rocket AI: Reward Shaping

A Reinforcement Learning project demonstrating Reward Hacking and how to fix it.

📖 The Story

1. The Baseline

We trained a PPO agent using standard Gymnasium rewards. It learned to survive, but struggled to land perfectly between the flags, often just "parachuting" down safely off to the side.

2. The Hack (Reward Hacking) 🎈

We gave the AI a flawed rule: "+1 point every frame if you are upright and slow." Instead of landing, the AI learned to hover in the air forever to maximize its score, hitting the 1,000-step time limit without ever touching the ground.

3. The Fix (Reward Shaping) ✅

We added a time penalty (-0.1 per step). Suddenly, hovering became expensive. The AI was forced to actually navigate, slow down, and land between the flags to claim the +100 landing bonus before the timer ran out.

🛠️ Technical Details

  • Environment: Gymnasium (Box2D Lunar Lander)
  • Algorithm: PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) via Stable-Baselines3
  • Training Steps: 3,000,000+ steps with custom reward shaping
  • Key Insight: "You get exactly what you reward — not what you want." This is a fundamental challenge in AI Safety.
