🚀 Rocket AI: Reward Shaping

A Reinforcement Learning project demonstrating Reward Hacking and how to fix it.

🎮 Try the Live Interactive App

📖 The Story

1. The Baseline: We trained a PPO agent using standard Gymnasium rewards. It learned to survive, but struggled to land perfectly.

2. The Hack (Reward Hacking): We gave the AI a flawed rule: "+1 point every frame if you are upright and slow." Instead of landing, the AI learned to hover in the air forever to maximize its score.

3. The Fix (Reward Shaping): We added a time penalty. Hovering became expensive. The AI was forced to actually navigate and land between the flags to claim the bonus.

🎥 Video Showcase

Watch the corrected AI successfully navigating and landing between the flags.

🛠️ Technical Details

  • Environment: Gymnasium (Box2D Lunar Lander)
  • Algorithm: PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) via Stable-Baselines3
  • Key Insight: "You get exactly what you reward — not what you want." This is a fundamental challenge in AI Safety.
